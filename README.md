How to run it:

Put `index.html`, `style.css` and `script.js` in one folder and open `index.html`. That's it.

What it can do:

- Write letters with a mood, category and tags
- Lock them until a date you pick (or repeat them weekly, monthly or yearly)
- Protect a letter with a password
- Attach a photo, or dictate with your voice
- Search, filter, favorite and bulk delete (with undo)
- Calendar view, stats and a writing streak
- Back up and restore your letters, export to CSV, share a letter as an image
- Dark mode and a few accent colors

Shortcuts:

`/` search · `n` new letter · `c` calendar · `Ctrl/Cmd + Enter` lock letter · `Esc` cancel editing

Good to know:

- Letters are saved in your browser on your device only. If you clear your browser data, they're gone, so use **Back up letters** now and then.
- If you forget the password on a protected letter, it can't be recovered.
- The PIN lock is just a privacy screen, not real encryption.
- Voice dictation works best in Chrome or Edge.

Files:

- `index.html` – the page
- `style.css` – the look
- `script.js` – everything that makes it work
